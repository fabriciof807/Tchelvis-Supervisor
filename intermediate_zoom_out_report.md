### 2. [Major] Multi-Channel Transport Sprawl, Lease-Heartbeat Churn, and Subprocess Contradiction
* **Affected Scope:** `High-Level Architecture` ([cockpit.md:93-101](file:///home/ubuntu/.t3/projects/cockpit/docs/designs/cockpit.md#L93-L101), [cockpit.md:147-151](file:///home/ubuntu/.t3/projects/cockpit/docs/designs/cockpit.md#L147-L151)), `Alternative C` ([cockpit.md:191-195](file:///home/ubuntu/.t3/projects/cockpit/docs/designs/cockpit.md#L191-L195)), `src/main.rs` ([cockpit.md:266-269](file:///home/ubuntu/.t3/projects/cockpit/docs/designs/cockpit.md#L266-L269)), `src/daemon.rs` ([cockpit.md:358-361](file:///home/ubuntu/.t3/projects/cockpit/docs/designs/cockpit.md#L358-L361)), `assets/herdr-app/mods/cockpit/main.js` ([cockpit.md:368-376](file:///home/ubuntu/.t3/projects/cockpit/docs/designs/cockpit.md#L368-L376))
* **Simplicity Rationale:** Mitigating the `<256 KiB` `hc` doc cap and stale-state risk during Critic/Comprehension stages introduced substantial transport complexity:
  1. **Three competing IPC modes:** `hc` `doc.set` CAS projection (`FleetSummaryProjection`), a custom Unix socket (`cockpit.sock`) serving `status`/`inspect` AND an unused "incremental NDJSON deltas" stream (`cockpit watch --json`), plus an inline single-cycle execution path inside `cockpit status` (overlapping with `cockpit daemon --once`).
  2. **Lease-renewal write amplification:** Storing a time-bound `lease_expires_at` in `hc` `doc.set` forces the daemon to periodically write `doc.set` just to extend the lease even when zero agent state changed, waking `doc` subscribers continuously and undermining event-gated operation.
  3. **Self-contradiction in `main.js`:** `Alternative B` ([cockpit.md:188](file:///home/ubuntu/.t3/projects/cockpit/docs/designs/cockpit.md#L188)) rejects spawning CLIs from `main.js` due to UI frame drops, yet [cockpit.md:99](file:///home/ubuntu/.t3/projects/cockpit/docs/designs/cockpit.md#L99) and [cockpit.md:372-374](file:///home/ubuntu/.t3/projects/cockpit/docs/designs/cockpit.md#L372-L374) require `main.js` to spawn `cockpit inspect <address> --json` on every row selection and `cockpit status --json` on lease expiry.
  Drop the speculative NDJSON delta protocol in `cockpit watch --json`, unify `--once` vs. inline `status`, and avoid periodic `doc.set` lease heartbeats (clear on graceful shutdown and probe `cockpit.sock` / `daemon.lock` only on demand).

### 3. [Major] Duplicate `agent_history` Persistence and V1 Provider/Operational Sprawl
* **Affected Scope:** `Goals` ([cockpit.md:33-35](file:///home/ubuntu/.t3/projects/cockpit/docs/designs/cockpit.md#L33-L35)), `src/store.rs` ([cockpit.md:318-321](file:///home/ubuntu/.t3/projects/cockpit/docs/designs/cockpit.md#L318-L321)), `src/providers/*` ([cockpit.md:323-356](file:///home/ubuntu/.t3/projects/cockpit/docs/designs/cockpit.md#L323-L356)), `src/setup.rs` ([cockpit.md:386-391](file:///home/ubuntu/.t3/projects/cockpit/docs/designs/cockpit.md#L386-L391)), `src/doctor.rs` ([cockpit.md:394-397](file:///home/ubuntu/.t3/projects/cockpit/docs/designs/cockpit.md#L394-L397))
* **Simplicity Rationale:**
  * `src/store.rs` introduces `agent_history(address, snapshot_json, updated_at)` in bundled SQLite (`rusqlite` C compilation + `PRAGMA user_version=1` + 72h pruning), directly duplicating `herdr-core`'s `hc ls --all --json` history and violating the core rule in [concepts_brief.md:29](file:///home/ubuntu/.t3/projects/cockpit/concepts_brief.md#L29) to treat `hc` as the sole source of truth for terminal state.
  * Despite Stage 5 Blocker #2, V1 still builds 6 full provider stacks across 24 target files—including dual REST + GraphQL + CLI fallback paths in `github.rs` and `gitlab.rs`, `DeployReleaseRef` ingestion, per-provider token buckets, dual GitHub/Linear quota-header state machines, Slack PR-body scraping, and YAML frontmatter runbook parsing—plus extras like `cockpit logs`, `cockpit doctor --bundle`, `--set-default-layout`, and `systemd` unit generation. Trim `store.rs` (remove `agent_history`; evaluate whether a simple in-memory/JSON ETag cache suffices in V1 instead of bundled SQLite), keep `gh`/`glab` and Jira/Linear/Slack adapters minimal, and defer secondary operational flags (`--bundle`, `systemd` generation, `cockpit logs`) until the core pipeline works.

* **Simplicity Rationale:** `src/correlate.rs` and `AgentRow` already infer `agent_kind` from `hc`'s native `foreground.argv` and `agent` binary names ([cockpit.md:157](file:///home/ubuntu/.t3/projects/cockpit/docs/designs/cockpit.md#L157)), and `hc` already tracks `reported` (`OSC 7501`).
